Thurston Alpine Frontend v1.1.5
=================================

Change:
- Reduced the oversized page title on the Pricing and Availability & Booking pages on desktop/laptop widths.
- Tightened the vertical padding around those page introductions so the pricing table and booking controls appear sooner.
- Slightly reduced the pricing feature figures on desktop.
- Changes are scoped to screens wider than 900px; the existing mobile/tablet typography rules remain unchanged.
- Includes the v1.1.4 homepage-link improvements.

Deployment:
Replace the existing frontend files with this package in your current GitHub repository, then commit and release as usual.
