Privacy Policy

This notice explains what personal information Thurston Alpine uses when you contact us or enquire about a snowboard lesson.

Who is responsible?

Thurston Alpine, operated by Richard Owens-Thurston, is responsible for handling the personal information described here. Contact: info@thurston-alpine.ch.

What information may be collected?

When you submit a lesson enquiry, we may collect your name, email address, phone number if provided, riding level, message, and the session, date, time, group size and price you select. Basic technical information may also be processed by the website host when you visit the site.

Why is it used?

Your information is used to respond to enquiries, check and organise lesson availability, manage bookings, contact you about your session, and keep appropriate business records. We do not sell your personal information.

Where is it processed?

The website is published through GitHub Pages. Booking information may be handled through the booking service connected to the website, Google Calendar for scheduling, and email services used to communicate with you. These providers may process data on systems outside Switzerland. Their own privacy terms and safeguards may therefore also apply.

Payments

Payment for lessons is taken in cash on the day. This website does not ask for card details or take online payments.

How long is information kept?

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond to your enquiry, organise and administer the lesson, handle follow-up matters, and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Calendar and email records may remain in the relevant service until they are deleted in line with the applicable account and retention practices.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect personal information. However, no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please avoid including sensitive information in the booking message.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask for deletion where we are not required or entitled to retain it. You can also contact us with questions or concerns about how your information is handled.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Richard Owens-Thurston at info@thurston-alpine.ch or call +41 76 229 77 53.
